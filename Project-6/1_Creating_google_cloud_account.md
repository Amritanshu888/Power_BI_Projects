# Google Cloud – Creating a Free Google Cloud Account

## 1. Objective of the Session

The purpose of this session is to create a **free Google Cloud account**, which will be used later in the project.

The upcoming workflow will involve:

1. Creating a Google Cloud account.
2. Loading data into **Google Cloud**.
3. Connecting Google Cloud with **Power BI**.
4. Creating a **Power BI report** using the data stored in Google Cloud.

---

# 2. Accessing Google Cloud

The instructor mentions that the Google Cloud registration link will be provided in the **Resources section** of the course.

### Steps

1. Open the Google Cloud registration webpage using the provided link.
2. Click on **Get Started for Free**.
3. The registration process may take some time, so wait if the page takes a while to load.

---

# 3. Select Country and Provide Email

After clicking **Get Started for Free**, Google asks for some basic information.

### Steps

1. Select your **country of residence**.
2. Enter your **email address**.
3. Accept Google's terms by selecting:

   * **Agree and Continue**
4. Proceed to the next step.

---

# 4. Create a Payment Profile

Even though the account is being created as a **free Google Cloud account**, Google requires billing/payment profile information.

### Steps

1. Google will ask you to create a **Payment Profile**.
2. Click:

> **Create new payment profile**

3. You will then need to provide your organization details.

---

## 5. Select Organization Type

Google asks:

> **What type of organization are you?**

For this example, the instructor selects:

> **Individual**

So, choose **Individual** if you are creating the account for personal/learning purposes.

---

# 6. Enter Legal Name

Next, provide the **legal name** associated with the payment profile.

The instructor gives an example:

> `B Test Tableau UK`

You can provide an appropriate name based on your own details.

### Important

The instructor emphasizes that you can provide **any suitable name** here and it is not an issue for this demonstration.

---

# 7. Enter Address Details

You also need to provide your address information.

The payment profile requires details such as:

* Address
* City
* Pincode / ZIP code
* State

### Steps

Fill in the required address information.

Then click:

> **Create**

Google will create the payment profile based on the information provided.

---

# 8. Add a Payment Method

After creating the payment profile, you need to add a payment method.

### Steps

1. Click:

> **Add Payment Method**

2. The instructor chooses:

> **Debit or Credit Card**

3. Enter your card details.
4. Click:

> **Save Card**

The instructor mentions that the **billing address and legal address are the same**, so the same address can be used for billing.

Then click:

> **Save Card**

---

# 9. Start the Free Google Cloud Account

After adding the payment method:

1. Click:

> **Start Free**

2. Google may ask you to verify the transaction/account.
3. Click:

> **Verify**

---

# 10. OTP Verification

Google sends an **OTP (One-Time Password)** to the registered mobile number.

### Steps

1. Check the OTP sent to your registered number.
2. Enter the OTP in the verification field.
3. Submit the OTP.

> **Note:** The lecture transcript contains an actual OTP. Do not reuse or record that OTP—it was only part of the instructor's live demonstration.

The verification process may take some time.

---

# 11. Provide Additional Information

After verification, Google may ask some additional questions about how you intend to use Google Cloud.

One question is:

> **What best describes your organization needs?**

For the demonstration, the instructor selects:

> **Startup**

You can choose the option that best represents your own situation.

---

## 12. Provide Reason for Using Google Cloud

Google may then ask you to provide a reason/use case.

The instructor indicates that you can provide:

> **Any suitable reason**

depending on your requirements.

---

# 13. Select Your Role

Google may also ask about your **role**.

### Step

Choose any appropriate role that matches your situation and then click:

> **Done**

---

# 15. Key Points to Remember

### Google Cloud account setup

* The Google Cloud registration link is available in the course's **Resources section**.
* Start the process using **Get Started for Free**.
* You need to provide your **country and email address**.
* A **payment profile** needs to be created.
* For an individual learner, the organization type can be selected as **Individual**.
* Provide the required **legal name and address**.
* Add a **debit or credit card** as the payment method.
* Complete the **OTP verification**.
* Provide the requested organization/use-case information.
* Select an appropriate **role**.
* Finish the setup by clicking **Done**.

### Project context

The Google Cloud account created in this session will be used in subsequent sessions for:

**Google Cloud → Data Loading → Power BI Connection → Power BI Report Creation**
